Quick Help / FAQ

How can I track my order?
You can track your order from the Track Order page. Enter your Order ID, Customer Name, or Tracking Code to view your order status and latest updates.
How do I retry failed payment?
You can retry a failed payment by clicking on the order in your Order History and selecting "Retry Payment", or contact our customer support for assistance.
How long does delivery take?
Deliveries within Mogadishu typically take 24 to 48 hours depending on your district and courier availability.
How can I change my address?
To change your shipping address, please contact our support team immediately before the order status updates to "Out for Delivery".
How can I return a product?
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unused products in their original packaging. Please submit a support ticket to initiate the process.
